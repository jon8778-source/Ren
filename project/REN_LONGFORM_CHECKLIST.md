# Long-form production checklist

Copy this checklist into each episode's production record; record dates, hashes,
evidence, outstanding issues and explicit owner decisions rather than ticking
items merely because files exist.

- [ ] Read `REN_LONGFORM_BASELINE.md`, `REN_VIDEO_RULES.md` and current project instructions.
- [ ] Research and source factual claims; compute and label illustrative figures.
- [ ] Save final script and obtain explicit owner approval before synthesizing voice.
- [ ] Inventory assets and provenance; verify Ren/supporting character continuity,
      clip durations, motion direction, missing poses and approval status.
- [ ] Create a shot plan: story action, genuine Ren animation, environments,
      transitions, financial labels and narration timing. Plan natural Ren returns.
- [ ] Produce approximately 60 seconds using the actual production workflow.
- [ ] Check the entire pilot, mobile readability, financial accuracy, audio and cuts;
      document limits and obtain explicit owner approval before full production.
- [ ] Preserve input hashes, approved assets, original audio and reusable caches;
      continue the complete film without unapproved substitutions or speed changes.
- [ ] Verify framing, plate perspective, grounded feet, contact shadows, furniture
      layering, native-resolution matting, white clothing and smooth motion.
- [ ] Check scene continuity, frame counts, half-open cut boundaries and the frames
      immediately before and after every transition; decode the whole output.
- [ ] Verify code-drawn financial figures, qualifications and narration coverage;
      review complete audio, levels and synchronization, and disclose listening limits.
- [ ] Inspect the complete film and every shot; save contact sheets and QA evidence.
- [ ] Deliver final video and report; obtain explicit owner final approval. Seek
      separate authorization before publishing or merging.
