## Published/primary references

- Marsh/Mercer, 2 September 2026, employer benefits survey: https://www.mercer.com/en-us/about/newsroom/employers-expect-health-benefit-costs-to-jump-in-2027/ . Average employer benefits cost per employee expected +8.2% in 2027; NOT guaranteed personal-premium growth. Earlier large-employer survey: approximately two-thirds expect to increase employee share.
- IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-24 (2027 limits): https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb26-25.pdf . HSA self-only annual limit $4,500, family $9,000; eligible HDHP minimum deductibles and OOP limits apply. Employer contributions count against contribution limit.
- IRS Publication 969: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969 . HSA eligibility, federal tax advantages and qualified distribution treatment; confirm state-specific tax rules separately.
- HealthCare.gov 2027 dates: https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/dates-and-deadlines/ . Nov 1 start; Dec 15 for Jan 1 effective coverage, Jan 15 final on HealthCare.gov at time checked; state deadlines and employer enrollment windows can differ.
- KFF, 30 October 2026: https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/9-things-to-watch-for-the-2027-aca-open-enrollment-period/ . 2027 finalized benchmark silver premiums average +13%; these are Marketplace plans, not Ren's employer plan.
- HealthCare.gov glossary: https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/out-of-pocket-maximum-limit/ . Out-of-pocket cap covers covered in-network services, excludes premiums and many uncovered/out-of-network costs.

**Result:** illustrative difference = $2,670 − $690 = $1,980 in net economic value, NOT a literal $1,980 immediate cash saving. HSA value must be shown separately from immediate cash costs. This assumes the employer funds the HSA and the employee is eligible.

PPO patient share assumes an example plan with $150 copays/coinsurance; HDHP patient's $600 share assumes the eligible covered in-network services cost $600 under that plan. It is an illustrative modelling choice, not proof that any two actual plans price care identically. Cost sharing, drug formularies, provider networks and cash-flow constraints must be independently checked.

HSA funding illustration: $140/mo redirected = $1,680 personal contributions; plus $750 employer = $2,430 contributions, under the $4,500 self-only 2027 cap. If $600 is withdrawn from HSA for care and there is no other activity, end balance $1,830, not $2,430. If medical bills are paid with non-HSA cash and no HSA withdrawals/fees/growth, HSA remains $2,430. Do not portray tax advantage as identical for every state.
