What If You Keep Postponing the Life You're Saving For?

Alex has a photo of a mountain taped to his fridge. He's been "ready" for four years, and every year there's a good reason to wait. Some of those reasons really are good: paying down the card, building emergency savings, saving for retirement. But what happens when "ready" keeps moving?

In this story, Ren follows Alex from careful saver to someone who can spend on purpose, without borrowing or touching his emergency fund. If your budget has no room right now, there's a version of this for you too.
