## S1: Hook, cold open (0:00-0:24, 11 shots)
Porch. Dev holds up a pay stub and asks the question that frames the whole video: "Who's keeping the difference?" Ren doesn't know what he means. Dev points at one line, glowing "Employer match: 2%", and Ren's stomach drops. Ren admits he turned down free money for three years, and did it to impress people he barely knew. He says "let me take you back", and a page-flip carries us to age 25.

## S2: Flashback, age 25 (0:24-1:05, 19 shots)
Office plate, "Age 25" lower-third. First real job, $44,000. He leases a brand-new car ($489/month) to look successful in the parking lot. HR hands over a folder; page six says the company matches every dollar up to 4%. He never reads page six and picks 2%, because 2% sounded responsible and the lease sounded urgent. A code-built card builds line by line, in sync: salary, lease, match.

## S3: The quiet years (1:05-1:48, 19 shots)
The mechanics of a match, shown with jars: You and Company both at 4%, then both at 2%, and a ghost jar marked "Never paid". Year counter 25, 26, 27 with "Missed: $880, $920, $960" and a fixed total of "3 years = about $2,760". Meanwhile the lease payment goes out on time, with pride. $489 to look successful against about $77 a month that would have made him successful.

## S4: Back on the porch, the reveal (1:48-2:22, 18 shots)
Dev's three questions appear as cards, the stub line goes from 2% to 4%, and the company keeps the difference because that is how a match works. At home, a code-built phone toggle goes from 2% to 4%, with a "90 seconds" badge. Ren: "Ninety seconds, three years late."

## S5: The sting, then the twist (2:22-3:06, 20 shots)
The painful maths: $2,760 on a 30-year timeline at a hypothetical 7%, a coin pile growing, and the sticker "about $21,000. Not a promise." Ren says the real cost isn't the payment, it's the opportunity. Then the twist: take-home only dropped about $32 per paycheck because contributions are pre-tax, and he never missed it. The $32 becomes "probably a car wash, for a car I didn't even own."
